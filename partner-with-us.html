Promote Our AI Tools

Some partners connect us to events. Some connect us to businesses that need a system. Others help get our AI tools into the hands of the Filipinos they were built for — students, SMEs, creators, and communities.

What you'd be promoting

⬡ Business Plan Studio SMEs · Students ⬡ Digital Readiness SMEs · Institutions ⬡ Brand Architect Professionals · SMEs ⬡ Content Machine Creators · SMEs ⬡ CarouselCraft Creators · Marketers ⬡ Bayanihan Hub LGUs · Communities

The tools are free to try, so a partner can send someone straight to the page and let the tool speak for itself — no demo call or setup required.

What promoting actually looks like

01

Tell us how you'd reach people

Social content, your own community or org, campus groups, business networks, LGU contacts — whatever channel you already have. There's no minimum audience.

02

We get you oriented

A walkthrough of what each tool does and who it's for, so you're never promoting something you haven't seen work.

03

You share, we handle the rest

Send people to the tools or route interested businesses back to us. We take it from there — onboarding, support, and follow-through stay on our side.
